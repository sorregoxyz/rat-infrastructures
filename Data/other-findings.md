# Other findings 

These new data were stabilized in 2026 from the same dataset created in 2021.

## Hunting works when it's collective

| Hunters   | Hunts | Rats killed | Success |
| --------- | ----- | ----------- | ------- |
| 1         | 4     | 1           | 25%     |
| 2         | 14    | 5           | 36%     |
| 3 or more | 11    | 9           | 82%     |

## Company changes how people react

When encountering rats alone, a person was the only one to flee in 68% of encounters. In pairs, that dropped to 35%,  and "both ran away" moved from 16% to 38%. One way of reading this is that people in company stand their ground a bit more.

## Rats in numbers hold their ground

In non-hunting encounters, when three or more rats encounter people, rats never fled alone. So group size works on both sides: more people make hunting succeed, and more rats make people leave.

| Rats      | People ran away | Rat alone ran away |
| --------- | --------------- | ------------------ |
| 1         | 49%             | 9%                 |
| 2         | 59%             | 4%                 |
| 3 or more | 67%             | 0%                 |
